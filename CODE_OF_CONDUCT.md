## Our Commitment

SSAR Group is committed to fostering welcoming, safe, and collaborative environments for all contributors, maintainers, and community members. We believe that diverse perspectives and inclusive participation strengthen our projects and community.

## Standards of Conduct

All participants in SSAR projects are expected to adhere to our [Open Source Code of Conduct](https://docs.ssar-group.com/legal/code-of-conduct?pr=opensource), which outlines the behavioral standards and expectations for community interaction.

## Label and Priority Guidelines

To maintain efficient workflows and protect maintainer resources, labels such as **Urgent**, **Critical**, **High Priority**, or similar classifications must be used appropriately and only when genuinely warranted.

### Appropriate Use

Critical or urgent labels should be reserved for:

- Security vulnerabilities requiring immediate attention
- Production-breaking bugs affecting multiple users
- Time-sensitive issues with significant impact
- Situations that genuinely require expedited review

### Enforcement Policy

Misuse of priority labels, including but not limited to exaggeration, repeated abuse, false urgency claims, or intentional disruption, is a violation of community standards and may result in enforcement actions.

Consequences for label misuse may include:

- Warning and request for correction
- Temporary restriction of labeling privileges
- Limitation of commit or contribution access
- Blocking of pull requests or issues
- Removal from contributor permissions
- Repository-wide ban in cases of severe or repeated violations

These measures are enforced to protect project integrity, prevent disruption, preserve maintainer attention for genuine priorities, and ensure fair treatment of all contributors.

For detailed information, refer to [Code of Conduct - Section 40.1](https://docs.ssar-group.com/legal/code-of-conduct?pr=opensource&lnk=401).

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, or if you have questions about appropriate community practices, please contact us:

- Email: [contactus@ssar-group.com](mailto:contactus@ssar-group.com)
- Subject line: `[CODE OF CONDUCT]`

All reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed promptly by the SSAR Group team.
